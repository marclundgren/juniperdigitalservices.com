FAQ

Most Frequently Asked Questions

Who will actually build my site?

Me. Juniper Digital Services is one person. You'll never be handed to a junior developer or an offshore team. The person who quotes the work is the person who writes the code and answers the phone afterward.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Small businesses of every kind. Retail shops, dental and medical practices, studios, trades and service businesses. Usually somewhere between one and thirty employees, with a site that's five or more years old.

How much does a website cost?

You get one fixed price before any work begins. It depends on how many pages you need, and whether the site has to do anything beyond inform. Forms, booking, intake paperwork. No hourly billing, no surprise invoices.

How long does it take?

Most sites go live within a few weeks of starting. You'll see a real working link in the first week rather than a slide deck, and two rounds of changes are built into every project.

Do I own the site when it's finished?

Completely. The domain, the hosting account and the code are all in your name. Nothing is rented from me, and if you ever want to take it elsewhere you can leave with all of it.

Can I update the site myself afterward?

If that matters to you, yes. I can wire up a simple editor so you can change text, hours and photos without calling anyone. If you'd rather not touch it, small updates are a flat fee.
